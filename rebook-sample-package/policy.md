# Halcyon Analytics — Domestic Air Travel Policy (rev. 2026-03)

## 1. Fare classes

Employees book Basic, Main Cabin, or Premium Economy. First and Business class are not reimbursable on domestic routes regardless of fare, loyalty status, or upgrade availability.

## 2. Spend authorization

A one-way domestic itinerary with a **total charged amount of $650.00 or less** (base fare plus all taxes, carrier fees, and seat fees) may be booked without approval.

Any itinerary whose total charged amount exceeds $650.00 requires written pre-approval from the employee's cost-center manager before booking. Bookings made over the threshold without pre-approval are the employee's personal expense. The threshold applies to the amount charged at booking, not the advertised fare.

## 3. Routing

Non-stop is preferred. One connection is permitted when it is at least $120 cheaper than the cheapest compliant non-stop or when no compliant non-stop exists. Two or more connections are not permitted. Layovers over 90 minutes are discouraged; layovers over 3 hours are not permitted.

## 4. Disruptions

When a carrier cancels a flight, the employee or their assistant may rebook on any carrier under this policy. The original fare is refunded by the carrier and does not offset the threshold in §2.

## 5. Medical and accessibility accommodations

Seat requirements recorded in the traveler profile as medical are mandatory. A fare class that cannot guarantee the required seat (for example, Basic fares with no seat selection) does not satisfy the requirement even if a seat happens to be available at check-in.

## 6. Holds

Seat holds are permitted for up to 30 minutes and are not bookings. An itinerary is booked only when a confirmation number has been issued.
